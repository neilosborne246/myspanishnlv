# SEO Audit — myspanishnlv.com

**Date:** 30 May 2026
**Scope:** Full site audit (369 URLs in sitemap; 21 root pages + 245 blog posts + 7 country pages + 9 requirements + 7 application-process + 13 rejection + 23 renewal + 7 consulates + 12 thematic hub pages)
**Auditor brief:** Spain Non-Lucrative Visa specialist service. €1,499 new application / €699 renewal. Operated by Retail Consulting UK Ltd in partnership with Platinum Legal Spain.

---

## Executive Summary

The site has a **strong content foundation** — 369 indexed URLs, well-structured topic hubs (requirements, application-process, rejection, renewal, by-country), genuinely substantial body copy on every key landing page (2,000–4,300 words), FAQPage schema on every commercial page, HowTo schema on `/how-it-works`, and Service + Offer schema on pricing. Transparent milestone pricing (€400/€400/€699) is a real competitive edge — most direct competitors hide their fees.

But there are **three critical, indexation-grade problems** that almost certainly explain any underperformance vs. potential, and **all three are fixable this week**:

1. **Canonical tags point to URLs that 301-redirect.** Sitemap and canonicals say `/pricing/`, `/what-is-nlv/`, etc. are canonical. Server returns `HTTP 301 → /pricing.html`. Telling Google "this URL is canonical" while the server says "no, the canonical is over there" wastes crawl budget and dilutes signals. The redirect chain works (so pages aren't 404ing) but every internal link/canonical/sitemap entry triggers an unnecessary redirect. Worse: **`/requirements/` returns HTTP 403** (folder index forbidden, no redirect configured) — so the canonical URL on `/requirements.html` literally points to a broken address. Fix this and rankings should improve within weeks.
2. **The blog has AI-template pollution.** A spot-check of 5 random blog posts all contained the same generic Mad-Libs FAQ block: *"What are the most common mistakes people make regarding this aspect?"*, *"How is this aspect handled differently by different consulates?"* — vague, repetitive, no real answers. Across 245 posts, this is exactly the pattern Google's Helpful Content system penalises sitewide.
3. **All 28 Open Graph images are the same byte-identical file** (MD5 confirmed). Every page reports a unique `og-*.jpg` URL but the file behind every URL is identical. Social shares look generic, and you're losing the social-CTR signal Google increasingly considers.

**Overall assessment: strong foundation, two critical issues holding it back.** Resolve the URL/canonical mismatch and clean the blog, and this site is positioned to outrank Lexidy, Costaluz, IAS, and Total.law for the specialist-service queries that actually convert.
