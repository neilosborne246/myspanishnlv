A Core Visa Requirement

Start With the Application Requirements

Official guidance provides for qualifying public or private health insurance. If you are considering private cover, check the insurer’s authorisation to operate in Spain and the policy’s benefits, restrictions and documents against your competent consulate’s instructions.

Checks Before You Rely on a Policy

  • Public or private — official guidance refers to public or private health insurance; any public entitlement is assessed individually
  • Authorised insurer — a private policy should come from an insurer authorised to operate in Spain
  • Your consulate’s instructions — check the cover, restrictions and documents your competent consulate asks for
  • The actual policy terms — copayments, waiting periods, limits, medical exclusions and cover dates
  • Issued evidence — the certificate names the correct people and dates before you submit
  • Continuing cover — keep cover in place for your stay; renewal applications ask for it again

Policy Terms

Review the Actual Offer

Ask about copayments, waiting periods, limits, medical exclusions and the required cover dates. A product name or a generic visa label cannot replace a check of the policy and your individual terms.

🚫

Copayments (Copago)

Some private policies charge a copayment each time you use a service. Check what your consulate’s instructions say about copayments before choosing a policy. The policies we arrange for NLV applications are issued without copayments, and the policy terms confirm this.

🏥

Benefits and Limits

Compare the policy’s benefits, limits, waiting periods and exclusions with the cover your consulate asks for. Accident-only or emergency-only cover is a different product from a medical policy for residence.

🇪🇸

Cover in Spain

Check that the policy covers you in Spain for the required dates, and that the certificate states this. If you are relying on an international policy, confirm that Spain is a covered territory and that the insurer can issue the evidence your consulate asks for.

📅

Continuous Cover

Keep your cover in place for the period of your stay. Renewal applications ask for health insurance again, so avoid gaps when you change payment method or policy.

✅

Authorised Insurer

Check that a private policy is issued by an insurer authorised to operate in Spain. Ask for the insurer’s name as it appears on the policy documents.

📄

Certificate Details

Check which language your consulate asks for, which details the certificate must show and whether proof of payment is needed. Requirements vary between consulates, so follow the instructions for the office responsible for your application.

Other Cover

Other Cover: Check It Before You Rely on It

Before you buy a new policy or rely on cover you already have, check it against your competent consulate’s instructions and the actual policy evidence.

Travel Insurance

Travel insurance is designed for trips. Don’t buy a travel policy as a substitute for checking the residence insurance requirement: the applicable official checklist governs the evidence. Ask for the policy wording and certificate rather than relying on a product’s marketing name.

Public Health Entitlement

Official guidance refers to public or private health insurance. If you believe you have a relevant public entitlement, have its evidence checked for your application. Don’t assume that every applicant must buy the same private product, or that a public entitlement applies to you without checking.

EHIC / GHIC Cards

The EHIC and the UK GHIC cover necessary treatment during temporary stays. They are travel cards, not evidence of a public entitlement as a resident. If you have a public entitlement, it is evidenced by different documents, which need checking for your application.

International Policies

If you hold an international policy, check that Spain is a covered territory for the required dates and that the insurer can issue the evidence your consulate asks for, in the language it asks for.

Home-Country Insurance

Domestic plans are usually designed for residence in your home country. Check whether your plan covers you as a resident of Spain for the required period, and whether the insurer can issue the evidence your consulate asks for.

Policies With Copayments

A policy with copayments can look comprehensive in every other respect. Check your consulate’s instructions on copayments before relying on one, and ask for the policy terms rather than a summary.

Choosing Your Cover

Choosing Suitable Cover for Your NLV

Every policy we quote for the NLV comes with a certificate for your application. These are the points to decide — your quote sets out the exact policy terms.

Cover throughout Spain

The policies we quote for the NLV cover you throughout Spain without copayments for covered services, and come with a certificate for your file.

Cover when you travel

If you’ll spend time outside Spain, ask about cover abroad. It varies between policies, and your quote sets out what applies.

Your age and health

Policies have age limits for taking out cover, and each person answers a health declaration. We check eligibility before you apply.

Everyone named

On a family application, each person is insured and named on the certificate, so the household is quoted together.

Before You Submit

Quotation, Cover and Certificate

Distinguish quotation, cover and certificate

A quote is a proposed offer. Acceptance, payment, cover commencement and document issue are separate events. Check that the required issued evidence names the correct people and dates before submission.

Plan families and medical history early

Request a quotation for everyone needing cover. Disclose medical history accurately and wait for the insurer’s decision; a certificate should be read alongside the individual policy documents.

Keep the visa and insurance services clear

Your insurance premium is separate from the visa-service fee. Insurance acceptance and visa decisions are made by the relevant insurer and authority. My Spanish NLV does not promise either outcome.

Common Questions

Health Insurance for the NLV — FAQ

Can a public-health entitlement be relevant instead of private cover?

Official guidance refers to public or private health insurance. If you believe you have a relevant public entitlement, have its evidence checked for your application; do not assume that every applicant must buy the same private product.

What if I have a pre-existing medical condition?

Disclose it accurately to the insurer and obtain its underwriting decision. Compare the resulting terms with your consulate's requirements before relying on the policy. Neither a quotation nor a visa adviser can guarantee an insurer's acceptance.

Can I arrange cover for the whole family?

Request an itemised household quote showing each person, start date, premium and relevant terms. Check whether everyone has been accepted and is named in the required evidence; a quotation for the main applicant does not prove family cover.

Is an insurance quotation the same as a visa certificate?

No. A quotation sets out a proposed price and terms. For your application, check the issued policy evidence, insured names, dates and any required payment receipt. A quote alone does not establish that cover has been accepted or started.

When should I request the insurance certificate?

Plan for acceptance, payment and issue of the required documents before your submission deadline. Ask the insurance team to confirm the sequence for your case. Do not treat a requested quote or an intended policy start date as proof that the certificate is ready.

Can the certificate issue date differ from the cover start date?

They describe different events: when a document is produced and when cover begins. Check both, together with the visa timetable and the insurer’s acceptance. Ask for corrections before relying on a document with an unintended start date.

Does an insurance certificate prove that every medical condition is covered?

No. Read the individual policy documents and underwriting decision as well as the certificate. Ask the insurance team to explain any exclusion or limitation and have its relevance to your application assessed before relying on that cover.

How much will my NLV health insurance cost?

Request a current personalised quote for everyone needing cover. Age, plan, location and individual terms may affect the offer. Compare total premium, payment arrangements and policy terms separately from the visa-service fee.

Can an older applicant use any over-60s health plan for an NLV?

Do not choose by age label alone. Ask for an assessment that considers both entry eligibility and the cover required for your residence application. A general private-health plan marketed to older residents is not automatically suitable for an NLV.

Should I cancel my existing policy before applying to another insurer?

Keep existing cover in place while the replacement is assessed. Confirm acceptance, individual terms and the new start date before following the old policy’s cancellation process. This is particularly important where there is medical history or upcoming residence paperwork.
