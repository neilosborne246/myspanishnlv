Health Insurance for Your NLV Renewal

To renew your non-lucrative residence authorisation, you need to show that you have kept health insurance throughout your current permit and that your cover continues. This guide explains what the renewal office checks, how to renew or switch your policy without a gap, and how to get your renewal certificate — whoever handles your renewal.

Keep your cover continuous

The renewal rules ask you to have maintained health insurance during the permit you are renewing, and to continue it (RD 1155/2024, art. 64.2.c). Keep your policy running, and if you switch insurer, make sure the new policy starts before the old one ends.

Plan ahead: renewals are filed in the two months before your permit expires, so check your insurance dates when you start preparing.

What the Renewal Office Checks

Your renewal is filed with the competent oficina de extranjería through the government’s electronic channels. Alongside the application, you show that you:

  • Hold a valid non-lucrative authorisation (or are within three months of its expiry)
  • Have sufficient means for the two-year renewal period — in 2026, €2,400 per month for a single applicant plus €600 per family member, or savings of €57,600 plus €14,400 per family member
  • Have kept health insurance throughout your current permit, and it continues
  • Have children of compulsory school age in school
  • Have lived in Spain for more than 183 days in the calendar year
  • Have paid the renewal fee

Renewing Your Current Policy or Switching

Keeping the same policy

Most NLV holders simply keep the policy they used for their visa. Sanitas Residents Visado and Residents Platinum Visado are annual contracts that continue from year to year, which keeps your cover unbroken.

Switching insurer

If you switch, arrange the new policy to start before the old one ends, then cancel the old policy with the notice its contract requires. A new policy means a new application and health declaration, so check how any existing conditions will be treated before you cancel.

Your Certificate for Renewal

Prepare documents showing your current cover and that it continues for the period of the renewal:

  • An up-to-date insurance certificate naming you, with the policy number and cover dates
  • Proof of payment for the current period
  • If you switched insurer, documents showing the old and new policies with no gap between them

If your policy is with Sanitas through us, we issue your renewal certificate. About the NLV renewal certificate →

Family Members Renewing

Each family member renews their own authorisation and needs cover that has continued throughout their permit. We can quote and certify the whole household together, so everyone’s documents line up.

Public Healthcare and the Convenio Especial

Some long-term residents consider moving to Spain’s public system through the convenio especial, a paid agreement. Whether it can be used as the insurance for an NLV renewal depends on your circumstances — read changing to the convenio especial at renewal before cancelling private cover.

What Doesn’t Count as Health Insurance

EHIC and GHIC cards cover temporary stays, not residence, and travel insurance is designed for trips. Neither replaces ongoing health insurance for your renewal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need health insurance to renew my NLV?

Yes. To renew a non-lucrative residence authorisation you need to have kept health insurance throughout the permit you are renewing and to continue it (RD 1155/2024, article 64). Your renewal file includes documents showing the insurance for the period you want to renew.

What if my health insurance lapsed during my permit?

A gap means you cannot show insurance maintained throughout the permit, which the renewal rules ask for. Restart cover straight away and talk to us — or your adviser — before you file, so the renewal is prepared with the right documents.

Can I switch health insurance before my renewal?

Yes. Arrange the new policy so it starts before the old one ends, then cancel the old policy with the notice its contract requires (Sanitas annual policies need at least one month’s notice from the policyholder). Switching means a new application and health declaration, so check how any existing conditions will be treated before you cancel.

Do I need a new insurance certificate for my renewal?

You need documents showing your current cover and that it continues for the period of the renewal — usually an up-to-date certificate from your insurer plus proof of payment. If your policy is with Sanitas through us, ask us for your renewal certificate.

Where do I file my NLV renewal?

Renewals are filed with the competent oficina de extranjería, through the government’s electronic channels, during the two months before your permit expires. Filing within three months after expiry is still possible but may lead to a penalty.

How much does health insurance cost at renewal?

It depends on your age, your plan and who is covered. If you already have Sanitas Residents Visado or Residents Platinum Visado, renewing usually means keeping the same policy running. For a new or switched policy, request a personalised quote — we normally reply within 24 hours, often sooner.

Can I use an EHIC, GHIC or travel insurance for my renewal?

No. EHIC and GHIC cards cover temporary stays, and travel insurance is designed for trips, not residence. You need ongoing health insurance in Spain.

Do family members need their own insurance at renewal?

Yes. Each family member renews their own authorisation and needs cover that has continued throughout their permit. We can quote and certify the whole household together.
