What is BLS?

BLS International is an external service provider that some Spanish consulates use to receive visa applications. Whether you apply through BLS or directly at the consulate depends on the consulate — in Canada and Australia, for example, there is no BLS centre for Spain and you apply directly.

Book only through official channels. This page lists only the official BLS Spain portals published on blsspainvisa.com for countries covered on this site (checked October 2026). Always confirm on your Spanish consulate's website before booking, and do not use look-alike booking sites.

Need help? Visit our BLS Application Guide for step-by-step instructions on submitting your NLV application through BLS.
