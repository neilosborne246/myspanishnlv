NLV Health Insurance Certificate: What It Must Show

Your certificate is the document the consulate checks. Here is what it needs to state, when it’s issued, and how to get it ready for your appointment.

What the Certificate Shows

What Your NLV Insurance Certificate Needs to Show

Consulates look at your insurance certificate, not your policy booklet. Check each of these before your appointment.

Who is covered

Your full name as in your passport, the policy number and the insurer. On a family application, every person is named.

What is covered

Cover throughout Spain with no copayments for covered services, as your consulate requires.

When cover runs

The start date and the period covered. Many consulates also ask for proof that the first year is paid, so visa policies are normally paid annually.

Timing

Choosing the Start Date on Your Certificate

Have your certificate ready for submission. Agree the policy start date against your intended arrival and your consulate’s current requirements.

The certificate can show a future start date: the date it’s issued and the date cover begins are different. No copayments for covered services. Benefits, exclusions and authorisation requirements are set out in the policy.

Before your appointment, check that

  • every name matches the passport exactly
  • the start date matches your intended arrival and your consulate’s current requirements
  • the certificate states cover throughout Spain with no copayments
  • you have proof of payment if your consulate asks for it

At Renewal

Your Certificate When You Renew

Renewals are filed with the Oficina de Extranjería, not the consulate. RD 1155/2024, art. 64.2.c asks you to have maintained health insurance throughout your permit and to continue it — so keep your certificate for each policy year. If you change insurer, confirm acceptance and the new start date before ending your existing cover, and arrange the dates so there is no uninsured gap. Renewal insurance explained →

Common Questions

Questions We Hear Every Day

When is the insurance certificate issued?

Once your policy is accepted and paid. In most cases the certificate can be issued the same working day.

Do I need the certificate before my consulate appointment?

Yes. Have your certificate ready for submission. Agree the policy start date against your intended arrival and your consulate’s current requirements. The certificate can document a future start date.

Does every family member need their own certificate?

Every person on the application must be insured and named. On a family policy each person appears by name, as in their passport.

Is the certificate in Spanish?

Yes. The certificate is issued in Spanish, which is what Spanish consulates and immigration offices work with.

What do I need at renewal?

Proof that your cover has been continuous during your current permit and continues afterwards. If you switch insurer, keep the certificates for both policies so there is no gap.
