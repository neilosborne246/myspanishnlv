The Top 10 NLV Mistakes — Part One

These are the five most frequently encountered mistakes in NLV applications. Each one on its own is grounds for refusal or significant delay. Eliminating them from your application before submission is the single most important thing you can do. The mistake: Submitting an EHIC, GHIC, travel insurance policy, or a health policy with co-payments as proof of health insurance.

The Top 10 NLV Mistakes — Part Two

The Modelo EX01 must be completed fully, accurately, and signed. Common errors include leaving sections blank, using an outdated version of the form, entering information inconsistently with your supporting documents, and submitting without the applicant's physical signature. Always download the current form directly from the official Spanish government website immediately before completing it. Any document not in Spanish must have a certified translation attached.
